As the curtains rose on career season, MSSM welcomed esteemed sponsors with open arms throughout February and March. Among the list, CIMB emerged as the Platinum sponsor, igniting excitement and anticipation among aspiring professionals.

CIMB Group Holdings Berhad, a cornerstone of the financial landscape in ASEAN, stood as a beacon of opportunity for ambitious students. Headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, CIMB boasts a formidable presence across 15 Asian countries. Offering a diverse array of banking and financial services – from consumer and wholesale banking to Islamic finance, investment banking, and insurance – CIMB’s reputation for excellence preceded them.

Introducing their prestigious “The Complete Banker” program to MSSM members, CIMB representatives sparked a collective gleam of aspiration. With interview sessions set ablaze throughout the day at the Student Union, the corridors buzzed with the promise of futures intertwined with CIMB’s storied legacy. The air crackled with potential as eager candidates showcased their talents and ambitions, leaving an indelible impression on CIMB’s discerning recruiters.

As the sun set on a day of boundless opportunity, MSSM committee members found themselves at Chinatown @ Manchester, breaking bread with CIMB’s representatives and their esteemed scholars. Against the backdrop of lantern-lit streets, laughter mingled with the aroma of culinary delights, marking the culmination of a fruitful day with CIMB.

In the heart of Manchester’s bustling streets, amidst the hum of ambition and the promise of tomorrow, MSSM and CIMB forged connections that would shape the careers of tomorrow’s leaders. As the stars twinkled overhead, dreams took flight, and the journey toward success with CIMB began.
