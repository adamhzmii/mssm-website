On December 14th, MSSM’s committee and members gathered at The Nest in the Student Union for a joyful Christmas celebration. Jingle and Mingle brought warmth and holiday cheer to a cold winter afternoon, with everyone (well, some of us at least) dressed in festive Christmas colors that filled the room with seasonal spirit.

The afternoon kicked off with ‘CocoaMilk Connection’, a fun socializing activity where attendees enjoyed hot cocoa topped with marshmallows, along with pizza and cookies—the perfect treats for a winter day. Laughter and conversations flowed easily as friends caught up and new connections were made, setting a cheerful tone for the festivities ahead.

The highlight of the event was the ‘GingerCraft Challenge’, where teams received gingerbread pieces, icing, and candies to build the most creative and structurally sound gingerbread houses. The room filled with the sweet smell of gingerbread as participants worked together to design everything from cozy cottages to impressive mansions. The friendly competition brought out everyone’s creativity and Christmas spirit.

Adding to the excitement, 14 participants took part in a gift exchange, swapping presents and spreading holiday joy among friends. The afternoon ended with an awards ceremony recognizing the best and most creative gingerbread house designs, followed by a group photo session that captured all the wonderful memories made.

Jingle & Mingle 2025 was truly a celebration to remember, bringing together friends, festive fun, and the warm spirit of Christmas. Until next year, may your holidays be merry and bright! 🎄🎉✨
