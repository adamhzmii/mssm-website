The Virtual Pre-Departure 2024, hosted on 17th August, was an outstanding event that perfectly blended information, interaction, and inspiration for incoming students. The session was meticulously planned, with a smooth technical setup that ensured a flawless experience from start to finish. The program kicked off with a warm welcome by the emcees, setting an inviting tone that carried throughout the evening. Participants were guided through various informative sessions that covered everything they needed to know—what to pack, how to navigate their arrival, and settling into life in Manchester.

One of the standout aspects of the event was the interactive Q&A session, which allowed freshers to ask questions directly and receive personalized advice from experienced committee members. The event also featured engaging presentations from affiliates and sponsors, showcasing the vibrant student community they would soon be a part of. The team’s dedication was evident in every detail, from the engaging slides to the well-timed background music that kept the mood light and lively. The Virtual Pre-Departure was not just a briefing; it was a welcoming celebration that made freshers feel excited and reassured about their upcoming adventure. A massive thank you to everyone involved for making it such a memorable and impactful experience!
