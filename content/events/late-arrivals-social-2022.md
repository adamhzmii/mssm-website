Settling into a new city is never easy, especially when you’re coming all the way from overseas with nothing but a few suitcases. In light of select students only flying into Manchester to attend their second semester in person, MSSM decided to hold a Late Arrivals’ Social to jumpstart our newcomers’ university experience.

The aim of this social event was so that old and new students alike, regardless of their year of study, could form fresh camaraderie, ask burning questions they had in mind, and get a feel of how MSSM events usually play out. Snacks and confectioneries were enjoyed as our venue grew louder with each attendee that entered the room, students standing around in small groups exchanging names and courses, eventually branching out to further topics.

With the commencement of our second semester in sight, MSSM would like to wish all students the best of luck in their studies! Don’t forget to follow our social media pages to stay updated on all the highly-anticipated events we have lined up for the rest of the academic year.
