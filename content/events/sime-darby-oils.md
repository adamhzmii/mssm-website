With career week continuing, Sime Darby Oils saw an opening to expand their reach to the vibrant city of Manchester. With a strategic focus on engaging students who couldn’t attend the fair, Sime Darby Oils embarked on a mission to tap into Manchester’s abundant talent pool!

As Sime Darby Oils are our new Gold Sponsors, MSSM offered unwavering support to Sime Darby Oils in their quest to strengthen their workforce with dynamic and ambitious individuals. Leveraging their expertise and networks, Sime Darby Oils aimed to connect with students eager to explore career opportunities within the oil and gas industry, presenting them with a pathway to a fulfilling professional journey.

The day began with an interactive session led by Sime Darby Oils representatives, offering invaluable insights into the company’s operations, opportunities for fresh graduates, and the overarching work culture. Students eagerly absorbed this firsthand knowledge, gaining deeper insights into Sime Darby Oils’ values and aspirations.

Following this enlightening session, the focus shifted to the interview arena, where students had the chance to showcase their skills and potential to Sime Darby Oils’ discerning representatives. With passion and determination, each candidate seized the opportunity to shine, leaving a lasting impression with their unique talents and capabilities.

After the engaging sessions and interviews, participants were treated to a delectable meal courtesy of Sime Darby Oils themselves. The feast featured traditional Malaysian cuisine, provided by our Bronze sponsors, Chilli Tales, offering participants a taste of home even miles away.

Throughout the day, the collaboration between MSSM and Sime Darby Oils was palpable as they worked together to bridge the gap between aspiring talent and promising opportunities. As the day came to a close, filled with promise and potential, both parties emerged enriched by the experience, ready to embark on a mutually rewarding journey toward success.
