Jom Raya 2024 was a spectacular and festive celebration held on 7th May at Platt Field Park, bringing the spirit of Hari Raya to life with vibrant activities and community bonding. From the moment attendees arrived, they were greeted with a lively atmosphere filled with music, laughter, and the tantalizing aromas of delicious food. The event was a beautiful blend of tradition and fun, featuring a variety of activities that catered to all ages. Open karaoke sessions were a big hit, allowing attendees to sing along to their favorite Raya songs and adding a lively musical backdrop to the day.

The traditional games area was another highlight, where participants enjoyed nostalgic childhood games that brought out friendly competition and plenty of laughter. The ketupat weaving demonstration was both educational and engaging, offering a hands-on cultural experience that many attendees appreciated. Food, of course, was a major attraction, with dishes generously provided by Chilli Tales, ensuring everyone could indulge in the festive spirit with a delicious feast.

The day ended on a high note with a lucky draw and performance sessions that kept the crowd entertained until the very last moment. The organizing committee did an outstanding job of managing the event, from logistics to creating an inclusive and joyful environment. Jom Raya 2024 truly embodied the essence of celebration, community, and cultural pride, making it a day to remember for everyone who attended. We can’t wait to see what next year’s event will bring!
