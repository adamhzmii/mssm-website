On 12 October, MSSM hosted its very first Wellness Day: Boardgames Break! at The Nest, Students’ Union, inviting everyone to take a breather from the hectic pace of university life. 🌿

The goal was simple: unwind, laugh, and connect. The space was transformed into a cosy corner of warmth and good vibes, where students could step away from assignments and lectures for a while and just enjoy the company of friends — old and new. 🧸☕

Tables were filled with board games and card games, sparking friendly rivalries, unexpected alliances, and plenty of laughter. From intense rounds of strategic gameplay to chaotic party games, the room buzzed with lighthearted energy and stress-free fun.

Wellness Day wasn’t just about games — it was a reminder to slow down, recharge, and appreciate the small moments that make student life a little brighter. ✨

Here’s to more wellness days ahead, because sometimes, a good game and a good laugh are exactly what we need. 🤍🎲
