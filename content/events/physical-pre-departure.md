On 5th September 2026, MSSM hosted our Physical Pre-Departure session at UNBOXMOU, SS15! It was an afternoon packed with fun, games, and great conversations for both incoming freshers and returning students.

After breaking into groups, everyone kicked off the session with icebreakers before diving straight into game mode. From board game classics like *Secret Hitler*, *Catan*, *Splendor*, and *Exploding Kittens* to chaotic multiplayer fun on the Nintendo Switch with *Overcooked!*, the energy in the room was unmatched.

To keep everyone fueled, we served up fresh chicken nuggets and drinks, giving everyone a chance to relax, mingle, and ask returning seniors all their burning questions about life in Manchester.

We wrapped up the day with great memories, new friendships, and plenty of excitement for the term ahead! 🎲🎮✨
