On 18th October, MSSM hosted our Milo Meet Up 1.0 at The Hive. The atmosphere was relaxed and welcoming from the moment guests arrived at 7:30pm. With cups of rich, chocolatey Milo in hand, our members gathered around tables filled with board games and card games. People shared their favorite games, turning the evening into a fun game night where Monopoly Deal brought out friendly competition and Exploding Kittens caused lots of laughter.

What made the night special wasn’t just the games or drinks, but the friendships forming around every table. Old friends caught up while new members found their place in the MSSM family, all united by easy conversation and shared memories of home. As the evening ended at 9:30pm, a group photo captured the joy and togetherness that filled the room.

Milo Meet Up 1.0 was exactly what we hoped—a space for our members to relax after classes, feel at home away from home, and build friendships that we hope will last long after university.

Thank you to everyone who joined us Here’s to many more Milo Meetups to come — because nothing beats a good cup of Milo and great company. 🥛🤝✨
