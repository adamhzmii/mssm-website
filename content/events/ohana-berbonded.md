On 26 April 2026, MSSM members gathered at Club Academy, Students’ Union for Ohana Berbonded 2026 — the exciting finale of the Ohana Family Programme. The event brought together “parents” and “children” from different Ohana families for a night filled with games, food, laughter, and celebration after months of bonding throughout the academic year.

The evening began with dinner and a fun Kahoot session, where families competed and tested how well they knew each other. One of the highlights of the night was the Build-A-Shelter challenge, where teams worked together using straws, tape, cardboard, and other materials to protect an egg from breaking. The activity encouraged teamwork, creativity, and plenty of friendly competition between the Ohana families.

As the event continued, members enjoyed catching up with friends, sharing jokes, and celebrating the friendships they had built over the year. Awards were also given out to recognise the winning Ohana families and their efforts throughout the programme.

With around 46 participants attending, Ohana Berbonded 2026 successfully created a warm and welcoming atmosphere that highlighted the strong sense of family and community within MSSM.🎉🫶✨
