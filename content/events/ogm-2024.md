The Ordinary General Meeting (OGM) 2024, held on 4th May at Uni Place, was a thoroughly engaging and professionally managed event that showcased the dedication and teamwork of the organizing committee. From the outset, the meeting was characterized by its smooth execution and clear agenda, providing a transparent platform for members to discuss important motions, hear reports, and participate in the election of new committee members. The atmosphere was charged with anticipation as each candidate delivered their speeches, sharing their visions and inspiring confidence in their leadership.

The election process was meticulously organized, with a structured timeline that ensured each segment flowed seamlessly into the next. The motion debates provided an excellent opportunity for members to voice their views, fostering an open and democratic environment. Participants were actively engaged throughout, with efficient voting procedures that allowed everyone to have their say in selecting the new leadership team.

The event concluded with a celebratory photo session with the newly elected committee members, capturing the excitement of the moment. The positive energy in the room was palpable, as attendees left feeling motivated and optimistic about the future direction of the organization. The OGM was not just a meeting—it was a testament to the commitment and passion of all involved, highlighting the strength of the community and the importance of coming together to shape the path ahead. A huge congratulations to the new committee, and a heartfelt thank you to everyone who made this event a success!
