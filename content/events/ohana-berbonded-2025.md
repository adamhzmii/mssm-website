On 23rd March, MSSM came together at The Hive, Students’ Union for one of the most heartwarming nights of the year — Ohana Berbonded. More than just another event, this was the grand finale of our Ohana Family Programme, where members and freshers gathered to wrap up a year of laughter, teamwork, and shared memories. ✨

From the very beginning of the academic year, our Ohana families — made up of “parents” and “children” — bonded through challenges and activities that earned them points throughout the year. Ohana Berbonded was the moment where all that effort came to life in one big celebratory showdown. 🏆👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

The Hive was filled with chatter, cheers, and inside jokes shared between families who had grown close over the months. The night featured fun games, special recognitions, and of course, the announcement of the winning Ohana family. Every laugh, every high five, and every photo captured was a reminder of how strong the MSSM community had become. 💫

Ohana Berbonded wasn’t just an end-of-year event — it was a celebration of connection, friendship, and the spirit of family that defines MSSM. 🧡✨
