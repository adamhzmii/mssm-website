As the sun set, Bazaar Malam 2025 came alive with the aroma of Malaysian street food, the chatter of hungry guests, and the nostalgic tunes of ‘Balik Kampung’ playing in the background. Held during Ramadan, this wasn’t just about indulging in flavors from home—it was about breaking fast together and celebrating the warmth of community.

With an incredible lineup of vendors—Kia Kitchen, 292 Streets, Chilli Tale’s, Sweet N’ Spoiled, and Warung Kita—guests were spoiled for choice. From Nasi Kandar, Nasi Lemak, and Laksa JDT to satay and kuih-muih, every dish transported us straight back to the bustling pasar malams of Malaysia. To top it off, our society served Teh O Ais, the ultimate refreshing drink after a flavorful meal.

It was a night filled with full bellies, familiar faces, and a comforting sense of home. A big thank you to our vendors, committee, and everyone who showed up to make it such a memorable evening. We hope you left with happy hearts (and maybe some leftovers too!) 🌙🍴
