Every university student collectively agrees on one thing – our course is not easy. That’s where Ohana Ber-bonding comes in, a lifesaver for us warriors. Imagine this: MSSM members of the same course teaming up to conquer the villain (coursework). It’s not just a study group; it’s a support system, a place where questions are welcomed. Ohana Ber-bonding aims to help all, making the challenging coursework and life here in general just a bit less daunting and a lot more fun.

Ohana Ber-bonding acts like a family scheme, with 2 Ohana Parents and 4 or 5 Ohana Children from the same course. They hang out, share tips, and help each other out.

The event started with a quick bingo-style icebreaker. We threw in some pizza because, well, who doesn’t love pizza? Then, we wrapped it up with a final game where fun tasks were given to every group and the three groups that completed the most tasks well would win an attractive prize. It’s not just about studying – it’s about making connections, having a laugh, and enjoying some good food together. Ohana means family, and family means no one gets left behind or forgotten!
