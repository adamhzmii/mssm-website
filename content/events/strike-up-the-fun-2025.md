Bowling shoes laced up, competitive spirits ignited, and the neon glow of Tenpin Manchester Printworks setting the stage—it could only mean one thing: Strike Up the Fun was in full swing!

With six lanes ready for action, participants were split into groups of six, each team guided by two committee members who were just as eager to show off their (questionable) bowling skills. The stakes? A group prize for the highest average score and an individual prize for the best bowler of the night—so naturally, every frame mattered.

As the first rolls were sent hurtling down the lanes, the atmosphere was electric. The cool lighting set the perfect mood, making even the most dramatic gutter balls feel like a cinematic masterpiece. Between strikes, spares, and the occasional “let’s pretend that didn’t happen” moment, the lanes were filled with lively banter and friendly heckling. Some came in with serious game faces, others just hoped to keep the ball out of the gutter—but one thing was certain: the energy was unmatched.

Between turns, conversations flowed as easily as the bowling balls (well, for some), with new friendships forming over shared laughter and completely unverified bowling techniques. As the final scores were tallied, cheers erupted for the night’s top performers. Some walked away with prizes, but everyone left with a highlight reel of unforgettable moments.

A huge thank you to everyone who joined us for this incredible night at the lanes! Whether you bowled like a pro or, uh, just provided moral support, we couldn’t have asked for a better crowd. Until next time—keep those throwing arms warm! 🎳🔥
