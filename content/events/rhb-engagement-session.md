As the Malaysian Career Fair in London drew to a close, RHB seized the opportunity to extend their reach to the bustling city of Manchester. With a keen focus on engaging students who were unable to attend the fair, RHB embarked on a mission to tap into the rich pool of talent nestled within Manchester.

Embracing their role as Silver sponsors, MSSM wholeheartedly supported RHB in their endeavor to bolster their taskforce with bright and ambitious individuals. Leveraging their expertise and networks, RHB sought to connect with students eager to explore opportunities within the financial industry, offering them a pathway to a rewarding career.

The day commenced with an engaging session led by RHB representatives, providing invaluable insights into the company’s operations, offerings for fresh graduates, and the overarching work culture. Students eagerly absorbed this firsthand knowledge, gaining a deeper understanding of RHB’s ethos and aspirations.

Following this informative session, the spotlight shifted to the interview arena, where students had the opportunity to showcase their skills and potential to RHB’s discerning representatives. Armed with passion and determination, each candidate seized the moment to shine, leaving a lasting impression with their unique talents and capabilities.

Throughout the day, the synergy between MSSM and RHB was palpable, as they worked in tandem to bridge the gap between aspiring talent and promising opportunities. As the sun set on a day filled with promise and possibility, both parties emerged enriched by the experience, poised to embark on a mutually beneficial journey toward success and prosperity.
