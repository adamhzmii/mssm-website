For Malaysians, Milo isn’t just a drink—it’s a taste of home, a comforting sip of childhood memories. And at this year’s Milo Meetup, that warmth filled the air at The Hive in the Student Union, bringing together familiar faces and new friends for a night of laughter, games, and of course, plenty of Milo.

Excitement filled the atmosphere as soon as guests walked through the door. Guests started off the night holding cups of rich, chocolatey Milo which created the perfect atmosphere for fun to follow. Each person managed to relax and have fun through FIFA 24’s friendly competition or the strategic gameplay of Monopoly Deal while Exploding Kittens brought chaotic laughter to everyone. Easy conversation allowed old friends to reconnect while new members became part of the MSSM family.

As the night came to a close, a group photo captured the joy and camaraderie shared throughout the event. It wasn’t just about the games or the drinks—it was about the connections made, the stories exchanged, and the feeling of home away from home.

To everyone who joined us, thank you for making Milo Meetup 2025 a success! Until next time, keep the Milo flowing and the memories growing. ☕✨
