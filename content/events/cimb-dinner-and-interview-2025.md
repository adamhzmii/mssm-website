#teamCIMB went all out this year, bringing the CIMB Campus Takeover to new heights in the UK! From London to Coventry, Scotland to Manchester, CIMB made its presence felt, engaging with hundreds of talented Malaysian students eager to explore exciting opportunities within the company.

The energy was high as the CIMB Interviews kicked off at the University of Manchester, where students put their best foot forward in a series of insightful discussions and assessments. It wasn’t just about answering questions—it was about understanding aspirations, exploring career pathways, and connecting with a team that values ambition and growth. The interview sessions provided a unique opportunity for students to gain first-hand exposure to what life at CIMB is like, all while showcasing their talents and enthusiasm for the industry.

But what’s a Malaysian gathering without good food and even better company? As the day transitioned into evening, shortlisted candidates and members of Malaysian societies gathered at Kaya Malaysian Restaurant for an exclusive CIMB Dinner. Over a spread of authentic Malaysian dishes, conversations flourished—students got the chance to network with CIMB representatives in a relaxed setting, exchanging stories, career insights, and of course, plenty of teh tarik-worthy chats.

Beyond just interviews and dinner, the event was a celebration of connection and opportunity. Whether it was through inspiring career discussions, meaningful networking, or simply enjoying a taste of home, the CIMB Interview & Dinner 2025 was more than just a recruitment drive—it was a reminder that wherever Malaysians go, our support for one another remains strong.

A huge thank you to CIMB for making this possible and to everyone who participated—here’s to the next generation of #teamCIMB! 🚀🇲🇾
