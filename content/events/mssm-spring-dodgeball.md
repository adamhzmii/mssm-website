As the trees burst into bloom and the vibrant hues of cherry blossoms painted the landscape of Manchester, it was evident that spring had arrived in all its splendor. Traditionally, this season heralded a break for the society, a time to pause and revel in the rejuvenation of nature. However, this year, we decided to shake things up and embrace the energy of the season in a unique way.

With a spirit of innovation and a desire to foster camaraderie among our members, we organized a mini-dodgeball tournament. Transforming the serene beauty of spring into an arena of friendly competition, we invited participants to embrace the thrill of the game and revel in the joy of teamwork.

Amidst the blossoming trees and the gentle breeze of spring, teams gathered on the court with their eyes set on winning the grand. After a few hours of battling, a victor emerged as they approached our sports officer to receive their hamper.

As the tournament unfolded, friendships were forged, bonds were strengthened, and memories were made against the backdrop of Manchester’s resplendent springtime landscape. It was a refreshing departure from tradition, a celebration of the season’s vitality, and a testament to the enduring spirit of our society.
