On 24th September 2026, MSSM headed to Academy 1 for the annual UoM Welcome Week Freshers’ Fair!

Our booth was fully decked out in Malaysian motifs and photo displays highlighting our best event memories. To bring a taste of home to Manchester, we gave out nostalgic Malaysian childhood snacks and candies to everyone who gave us a follow on Instagram. A special shoutout to our partner, PasarMart UK, for sponsoring these delicious treats! 🍬🇲🇾

It was an incredible turnout—we officially welcomed 40 new Malaysian freshers to the MSSM family! Beyond our booth, our committee members also visited other society booths to share Malaysian culture and spread awareness about our vibrant heritage across the wider university community.

Thank you to everyone who dropped by to say hi. We can’t wait for an amazing year ahead together! 🥳✨
