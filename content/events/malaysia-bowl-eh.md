On 14th October 2026, MSSM hit the lanes at Wynwood Lanes for our very first bowling social of the year: Malaysia Bowl-eh! 🎳💥

The event title pays homage to the iconic Malaysian phrase “Malaysia Boleh!”—capturing our shared spirit of persistence, community, and making anything possible (even getting a strike on your first try!).

With 35 members taking over 5 lanes, the energy was high from start to finish. We had a great mix of seasoned players and absolute beginners—our committee members were right there to share tips and get everyone up to speed!

### Highlights of the night:

- 💨 Mist & Strikes: Every time someone scored a strike, the lanes burst with mist, sparking roars of excitement and laughter across the alley!
- 👶 The President’s Comeback: After a rocky start to his game, our President, Max, embraced the spirit of fun and brought out the kids’ ramp tool. The result? A legendary streak of spares that had the whole crowd in stitches!

After two action-packed games, we wrapped up an unforgettable evening. Huge thanks to everyone who came out to strike up new friendships! 🏆✨
