On 7 & 9 April, MSSM proudly hosted Engagement Season 2025, an exciting two-day event that brought some of Malaysia’s top companies — SD Guthrie, Gamuda, and EY Malaysia — right to the heart of Manchester.

The event kicked off on 7 April at Academy 3, Students’ Union, where SD Guthrie and Gamuda took the stage to share exclusive insights into their industries, company culture, and the exciting career pathways they offer. Just two days later, on 9 April, the spotlight shifted to the Samuel Alexander Building, where EY Malaysia delivered an inspiring session headlined by Lee Soo Fern, Talent Partner at EY Malaysia, who shared invaluable tips on standing out in the competitive job market. 🚀

Targeted at members and freshers, Engagement Season wasn’t just a talk series — it was a gateway for students to explore real opportunities back home. From graduate programmes to internships, these sessions gave students a clearer picture of what it means to kick-start their careers with industry leaders.

The atmosphere was buzzing with questions, ideas, and new connections as attendees engaged directly with company representatives during Q&A and networking sessions. It was the perfect platform to bridge the gap between Manchester and Malaysia’s professional landscape, leaving students inspired and better equipped for their future careers. 🌟🤝

Engagement Sessions 2025 proved that career exploration doesn’t have to wait — it starts here, together. 💼🇲🇾✨
