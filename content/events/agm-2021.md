The arrival of yet another Annual General Meeting witnessed the swearing-in of a new MSSM committee, with members running for positions of Event Coordinators, Sports Officers, Publicity Secretary, Welfare Officer, Media Officer, and Production Officer.

Decked out in formal wear, running candidates presented their manifestos and handled Q&A sessions to the best of their abilities before returning to their seats to await the results. Old and new MSSM members alike were present at AGM to cast their votes for the next committee to represent their interests and attendees were not shy to flag up imperative questions and issues that needed to be addressed.

With both fresh and familiar faces in the 2021/22 committee, MSSM aims to bring around another academic year’s worth of worthwhile events and exciting opportunities to its members.
