Spring Sports: Bouldering 2026 gave MSSM members an exciting chance to stay active, challenge themselves, and spend time with friends through an afternoon of indoor climbing. Held on 25 April 2026 at Rock Over Climbing Manchester, the event attracted 43 participants and became one of the most popular Spring Sports events of the year.

Throughout the event, participants tested their strength, balance, and problem-solving skills by climbing different bouldering routes designed for various difficulty levels. Many members, including beginners, enjoyed learning the sport in a supportive and encouraging environment. The climbing staff at the venue were also praised for being friendly and helpful from start to finish, helping participants feel more comfortable and confident while climbing.

The event was filled with laughter, encouragement, and memorable moments as friends climbed together, took photos, and supported one another throughout the session. Snacks and drinks were also provided, giving everyone a chance to relax and socialise between climbs.

Feedback from attendees was overwhelmingly positive, with many participants saying they would love to see more Spring Sports events in the future. Spring Sports: Bouldering 2026 successfully created a fun and welcoming atmosphere while encouraging members to try something new together.🧗‍♂️🪨🔥
