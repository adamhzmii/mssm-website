A fresh start to MNight 2023: Teh Tarik marks the first gathering of the entire team behind MNight 2023, a team consisting of over 100 people! The event was held for the producers to introduce this year’s MNight theme to the rest of the team and for the team to get to know each other.

Why Teh Tarik? The thing that binds us most is the common memories we share growing up, and what better than the memory of chatting whilst drinking Teh Tarik at the Mamak. We served our homemade Teh Tarik that the MSSM team worked tirelessly on to put out over 100 cups worth through the event. Of course, we didn’t forget food. We served our favourite Malaysian snacks from The Chilli Tales!

Over drinks and snacks, this free and easy event helped the MNight Team meet and bond for the long practices and nights to come leading up to MNight 2023.
