To give everyone a breather from the first semester as well as get in the jolly mood, MSSM’s Christmas Social was an open invitation for its members to dress up in their ugliest Christmas sweaters and show up for a night of festivities.

The night kicked off with some who-ho-ho-lesome rounds of Christmas Mafia where the grinch was the antagonist instead. Once the grinch has been found out in each group and the games have come to an end, the hot chocolate that had been brewing all this while was also ready to be served. As the sweet smell filled up Academy 3, participants formed a beeline for the refreshments station to get ahold of a piping hot cup themselves, along with a slice of pizza.

Our resident committee members also serenaded the participants with several Christmas jingles to liven up the mood, stepping away from the traditional tunes that everybody is oh-so-familiar with come every holiday season. This may very well mark the first time many freshers are celebrating Christmas away from home and we hope our event has lit a fire to warm their hearts.
