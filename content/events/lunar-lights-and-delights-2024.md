As the sun was shining bright over our beloved manny, The Hive SU came alive with the bright colours of lanterns, the comforting aroma of warm tea, and the unmistakable sweetness of mooncakes.

The evening kicked off with a hands-on lantern-making session, where guests got to channel their creativity into crafting their own beautifully lit masterpieces. Some took the traditional route, delicately assembling classic designs, while others went bold with vibrant colors and artistic patterns. No two lanterns were the same, but each one carried a special touch of personal flair.

Of course, no Lunar celebration would be complete without mooncakes and tea. Whether savoring the rich, velvety textures of the mooncakes or sipping on a warm, fragrant brew, every bite and sip felt like a comforting nod to home. It wasn’t just about the food—it was about sharing traditions, swapping stories, and enjoying the company of friends old and new.

As the event wound down, The Hive glowed softly with the warmth of newly crafted lanterns and the laughter of those who had spent the evening making memories. Whether guests came for the crafting, the treats, or just the good vibes, one thing was certain—Lunar Lights and Delights was a night to remember.

A huge thank you to everyone who joined us! Until next time, keep the lanterns shining and the traditions alive. ✨🏮
