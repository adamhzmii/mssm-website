On 3 May, MSSM hosted our Spring Sports: Pickleball Social at Sugden Sports Centre, bringing everyone together for a day of rallies, smashes, and laughter. Whether you were a seasoned pickleball pro or picking up the paddle for the first time, this event was all about sports, movement, and pure holiday fun. 🏃‍♀️🏃‍♂️

The courts were buzzing with energy as players went head-to-head in friendly matches, cheering each other on through epic rallies and dramatic saves. But more than just competition, the event was filled with wholesome vibes — lots of laughs, shared high-fives, and a real sense of community. 💪💜

From the moment the first serve flew to the final point, everyone was all in. For some, it was their first taste of pickleball, and for others, a perfect way to show off their skills — but for everyone, it was a smash hit of a day. 🏓🔥

A massive thank you to everyone who joined us and made the Pickleball Social such a success. Who knows? This might just be the start of a new MSSM sports tradition. 😉✨
