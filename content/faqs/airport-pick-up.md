## Will MSSM or the university provide a pick-up service from the airport? If there isn’t, may I know which is the best way to get to the university from Manchester airport?

The Airport Support Service to the University of Manchester for freshers arriving at Manchester Airport is available only from 13th to 15th September 2023, from 8:00am and 8:00pm UKT. Look out for them as you exit from your terminal!

If you are arriving outside of these dates and hours, the quickest way to get to the university from UoM is by Bolt and/or Uber (you can download these apps ahead of time; Bolt is usually cheaper). The fare will range from £16-25 and it should be about a 26-min drive.

If you would like £5 off your first ride (limited places), shoot our committee a message on Instagram for a referral code!
