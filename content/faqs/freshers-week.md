## What does freshers’ week look like? What are some events and orientations that will take place?

Throughout a typical freshers’ week, there will be online and physical induction events for students (may vary between courses). There will also be a freshers’ fair where clubs and societies will set up booths around campus to promote and recruit members, so do make sure to attend the freshers’ fair to scout for societies of your interest!
