## What are the different types of accommodation?

First, there are university halls:

- Apply through the university; view more info and photos on their [website](https://www.accommodation.manchester.ac.uk/).
- There are three main areas: *Fallowfield* (near the Fallowfield campus, far from the Main campus), *Victoria Park* (very near Main campus) and *City* (near Main campus and North campus).
- A variety of options: ensuite, shared bathroom, catered and non-catered.
- Decently priced, options depend on your budget.
- Able to meet fellow university mates and have opportunities to socialise during ResLife events.
- A good mix of local and international students.

Then, there are private accommodations:

- 2 types: student accommodation (private halls) or rented houses.
- Options depend on your preference; distance from campus, rent price, facilities, shops nearby, etc.
- Suggested locations: city centre, Brunswick/Victoria Park area, Rusholme.
- Private halls tend to be priced similarly to university halls (or slightly higher) and can be found in most areas.
- Can choose to live with people that you already know or be allocated randomly (they will ask for your preference).
- A good mix of local and international students, though they might not attend the same university.
- Popular choices among students: VITA students, Sanctuary Students, CRM Students, iQ Accommodation, Unite Students.
- Rented houses tend to be cheaper than student halls (university or private) and are located mainly towards the south of main campus; these can be searched for online or through MCOT.
- MCOT helps promote rooms/houses rented out by other Malaysians, typically in Rusholme as it is usually cheaper, has a lot of options for halal restaurants / grocery stores and close to other Malaysians.

## Which are the more affordable options?

Accommodation in Manchester is generally affordable. There is a variety of options to choose from depending on your budget and room type preference!

## If I’m studying accountancy at UoM, what is a comfortable place to rent that is also close to the university?

Somewhere near Brunswick or behind AMBS (basically near campus).
