## How do I join sports teams and clubs?

One way to join the university’s sports teams and clubs is by joining Sporticipate (UoM’s sport participating programme) through the Playwaze app or website.

You should also keep an eye out for MSSM’s sport socials! We have regular sessions for basketball, football, badminton, volleyball, netball, frisbee, and more. This is the prime activity for you to make friends who share your interests in sports.

## What sports facilities do students usually visit?

For aquatic-related sports, your best pick would be Manchester Aquatic Centre (next to the Engineering Building). For gym and other sports, we recommend Sugden Sports Centre (opposite the Engineering Building), Trinity Sports Centre (a short walk from the Uni Library), Armitage Sports Centre (in the Fallowfield Campus), JD Gyms Manchester, etc.

## How can I book sports facilities?

You can book sporting facilities (e.g. football and badminton courts) by booking through sports centres’ respective apps. For example, Sugden Sports Centre has the app *Better UK.*
